# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project exists to make programming concepts friendlier and more fun to
learn. We want everyone who uses it, whether as a student, a teacher, or a
contributor, to feel welcome, regardless of experience level, background, or
identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind, patient, and respectful. Everyone here is learning something.
- Give feedback that is constructive and specific, and receive it graciously.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and on helping each other learn.

## Respect for others' work

This project is open source, and you are encouraged to use, teach with, and
build on it. In return, we ask that you:

- **Credit the author.** Keep the copyright notice with the code (MIT License),
  and credit the slides and teaching materials as described in
  `LICENSE-CONTENT.md` (CC BY 4.0).
- **Say what you changed** when you share an adapted version.
- **Credit other contributors** whose work you build on.

Presenting or publishing this work as your own, without credit, goes against
the spirit of this community and the terms of its licenses.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or jokes
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  welcoming, educational setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, and discussions, and when someone is representing the project in
public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have concerns about
credit and attribution, please contact the maintainer at
**[your-email@example.com]**. Reports will be handled promptly and
respectfully.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation by anyone who repeatedly violates it.

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is written in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org), a widely used standard for open source
communities.
